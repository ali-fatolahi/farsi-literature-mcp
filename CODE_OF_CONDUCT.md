# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and constructive in discussions
- Giving and receiving feedback graciously
- Focusing on the technical and historical merits of an issue
- Respecting provenance, attribution, and source integrity
- Being patient with people who are learning or working across languages and cultures

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or abusive conduct
- Personal attacks or demeaning comments
- Trolling, disruptive behavior, or repeated off-topic comments
- Deliberate plagiarism or misrepresentation of source material
- Publishing private data or credentials without permission

## Enforcement responsibilities

The repository owner is responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code of conduct. Consequences may include a warning, restriction from participation, or removal from contributor access when appropriate.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies within project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community.

## Enforcement

Reports of unacceptable behavior should be sent to the repository owner privately. The project maintainer will review the report, determine an appropriate response, and communicate the result as needed.
